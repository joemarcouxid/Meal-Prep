Evidence base

Why the app works this way

Fuel for the work

Training and competition nutrition should match the athlete's workload, with attention to quantity, quality, and timing of food and fluids.

Eat regularly

Youth-athlete guidance emphasizes frequent eating so long school-day gaps do not create large within-day energy deficits.

Protect energy availability

Persistent under-fueling can impair health, recovery, bone health, reproductive function, and performance.

Food first

The app prioritizes ordinary foods. Supplements are optional and carry contamination and eligibility risk for competitive athletes.
